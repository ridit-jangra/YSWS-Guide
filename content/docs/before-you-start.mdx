## What is a YSWS?

Ok, so this is the basics of what a YSWS is, YSWS stands for `You ship, We ship` where you ships a project and hackclub ships a reward to you!

For example: 
Alice creates her first ever software project, her portfolio!
She submits her project to a YSWS, lets say [Pixl](https://pixl.hackclub.com), she spents 5 hours working on her portfolio and she tracks it with [hackatime](https://hackatime.hackclub.com) (we will talk about hackatime in next sections). She makes a project in Pixl, writes a name, a description and links the github repo link, the demo link and connects her hackatime project and then she submits it to Pixl!! 
Pixl then reviews the project and gives Alice pixels (or any other curreny the programs give), now she can spend these pixels (or coins) on the shop!!
The shop can contain many items like 3d printers, grants, merch and other stuff.

Thats how a YSWS works.

Running a YSWS on the other hand is a complete new experience, it comes with joy and pain and then joy again and then your become mentally unstable (joking)....

## Lets break down a YSWS into smaller chunks<br/>

### Tech stack
Hackclub doesn't limit you with any framework, you can just anything, e.g. [NextJs](https://nextjs.org/), [ReactJS](https://react.dev/), [Svelte](https://svelte.dev/), or anything you want.
like Pixl uses NextJS and Static HTML, [Alchemize](http://alchemize.hackclub.com/) uses Svelte.
Every Hackclub program, event, everything is opensource and available at [Hackclub Org](http://github.com/hackclub/)

### Hackclub Infrastructure
Hackclub infrastructure has its own page but we will just talk about the basics here.
Hackclub uses Orchards (they're own hosting platform) and you can also use [Vercel](https://vercel.dev) if you like that better.

### Reviews
Reviews also is a huge topic but just the basics here.
Reviews are essentially the most important part of a YSWS, without these you don't know what projects are people submitting, and folks will probably fraud 😭. <br/>

Reviewing a project is simple yet complex, you need to look at the reviewing guidelines first to know what a good review is, how to write justifications and how to know how much hours to approve.

A project can either be approved or request changes, an approved project is granted coins (or any other YSWS currency, in here we will just refer to coins) that the folk can spend on the shop. Request changes project on the otherhand is rejected for a particular or multiple reasons that are told to the user so they can fix what they did wrong, and if someone did fraud (we will talk about fraud in next sections) the project or even the user can be banned.

### Fulfillment
Fulfillment is as important as reviews.

Fulfillment is essentially just placing orders and giving out grants (grants are a HCB topic that we will discuss in next sections).
Right now you just need to know this much about fulfillments.
