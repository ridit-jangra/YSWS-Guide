## Posting Ads and getting folks in your slack channel
Now that we you have a pitch & a landing, we now need folks in your slack channel, having a community is pretty good and a sponsor might like an active community. <br />
The real question is how? You can post ads in [#present](https://hackclub.slack.com/archives/C01GPEL6CGM) and [#ysws-finder](https://hackclub.slack.com/archives/C07MGRE9Y8P) or even [#ysws-drafts](https://hackclub.slack.com/archives/C0B9KCZU13N) (*ysws-drafts* is not only for posting pitches, you can advertise your YSWS here by pasting the same pitch 🤭) <br/>

Now its not easy to get folks in your channel or even making the channel active, its a pretty hard job to do and you might not even reach 200 in your first attempt but its part of the process, if you YSWS is niche then its common to have less people, much vague YSWS have higher amount of people in its channel because of the catorgies of projects!

Having around ~50-200 for a niche YSWS is a really goood number, because most people don't like making really niche projects, but if your channel doesn't reach that many don't worry, sponsors have sponsered YSWS with less than ~20 folks in channel 🤩

## Finding a Sponsor
Now its a really important thing, because without a sponsor your YSWS is just really a draft and its not an easy task to do, its can be a really long wait (but if you're lucky then you can find a sponsor in weeks or even days).
You can't really DM a sponsor or find them wandering 😭 a gappie (a Hackclub gap year) sees messages in [#ysws-drafts](https://hackclub.slack.com/archives/C0B9KCZU13N), [#present](https://hackclub.slack.com/archives/C01GPEL6CGM), [#ysws-finder](https://hackclub.slack.com/archives/C07MGRE9Y8P) and many more channels, and if they really like your idea, they will DM you and ask questions about your YSWS like `what are your goals`, `why do you wanna make it` and much more that we will discuss in next chapters.
