## Finding a Sponsor
Now its a really important thing, because without a sponsor your YSWS is just really a draft and its not an easy task to do, its can be a really long wait (but if you're lucky then you can find a sponsor in weeks or even days).
You can't really DM a sponsor or find them wandering 😭 a gappie (a Hackclub gap year) sees messages in [#ysws-drafts](https://hackclub.slack.com/archives/C0B9KCZU13N), [#present](https://hackclub.slack.com/archives/C01GPEL6CGM), [#ysws-finder](https://hackclub.slack.com/archives/C07MGRE9Y8P) and many more channels, and if they really like your idea, they will DM you and ask questions about your YSWS like `what are your goals`, `why do you wanna make it` and much more that we will discuss.
